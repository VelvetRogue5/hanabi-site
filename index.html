Three quick steps

How to Play

1. Tap a rocket

Every rocket flies straight along its arrow. If another rocket blocks the path, it stays put, so look before you tap.

2. Turn the cube

Drag to rotate and pinch to zoom. Rockets hidden on the far faces come into reach once you turn them toward you.

3. Clear the night

Launch every rocket to finish the night, enjoy your firework show, and move on to the next city.

Good to know

FAQ

Do I need a login?

No. Your progress, unlocked fireworks, and settings are stored on your device.

Does Hanabi include ads?

Yes. The game is free to play and supported by ads: optional rewarded videos, interstitials between nights, and banners. It has no in-app purchases. The app also uses analytics, remote configuration, and install measurement, all described in the privacy policies.

Where can I read the platform details?

Read the iOS privacy policy or Android privacy policy, or visit Support.
