Full Description

Every night, the sky is waiting.

Hanabi: Light Up the Sky is a relaxing 3D arrow puzzle. Each night brings a cube packed with firework rockets. Tap a rocket and it flies the way its arrow points, but only if nothing is in the way. Find the right order, clear the cube, and watch every rocket bloom into fireworks above the city.

Turn the cube to reach rockets on the far side, pinch to look closer, and plan a few moves ahead. Some launches spell out words in sparks, some bring an aurora across the sky, and every cleared night ends with your own firework show.

Features:
• 3D cube puzzles, from gentle first nights to real brain-teasers
• One-tap controls: tap a rocket, watch it fly
• Rotate and zoom the cube to find every path
• 17 firework shapes to unlock, from Peony and Heart to Chrysanthemum and Dahlia
• Night skylines, famous landmarks, passing planes, and auroras
• A bulb that lights every rocket that can fly, and a broom for stubborn ones
• Soft-fireworks mode, music, sound, and haptic settings
• No account required
• Free to play, supported by ads
• Progress stored locally on your device

Free a rocket. Light up the sky.

Google Play Review Notes Draft

  • Account required: No.
  • In-app purchases: No. The app does not use Google Play Billing.
  • Notifications: None.
  • User-generated content / player communication: No.
  • Contains ads: Yes. Rewarded video, interstitial, and banner ads via AppLovin MAX, mediating AppLovin, Google AdMob, Unity Ads, Vungle (Liftoff), Mintegral, Pangle (ByteDance), BidMachine, and Bigo Ads.
  • Third-party SDKs: AppLovin MAX and mediation adapters; Firebase Analytics and Remote Config; AppsFlyer with the Play Install Referrer; Meta (Facebook) App Events. No Facebook Login. No crash-reporting SDK.
  • Advertising ID: Declared. The AD_ID permission comes from the bundled Firebase, AppsFlyer, and ad SDKs and is used for advertising, analytics, and attribution.
  • Level content: Level packs are delivered through Firebase Remote Config and Google Cloud Storage; bundled levels keep the game playable offline.

Data Safety Draft

Draft answers for the Google Play Data safety form, to be confirmed against the production app bundle and each SDK's Data safety guidance:

  • Device or other IDs — collected and shared. Purposes: Advertising or marketing, Analytics, Fraud prevention. Includes the Android Advertising ID, the Firebase app instance ID, the AppsFlyer ID, and the Meta anonymous ID.
  • App activity · App interactions (app opens, sessions, engagement) — collected and shared for Analytics and Advertising or marketing.
  • Approximate location — advertising partners may derive coarse location from the IP address; the app itself requests no location permission.
  • Personal info · Email address — only if the player chooses to send a support email; used for App functionality (customer support).
  • Encrypted in transit: Yes. Data deletion: players may request deletion by email; uninstalling clears local data.
  • Not collected: name, precise location, contacts, photos, financial info, purchase history, crash logs.

Data collection is not optional for the player, and the app is not designed for children, so the target-audience and content-rating answers must reflect in-app advertising.
