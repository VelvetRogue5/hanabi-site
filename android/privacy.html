Hanabi for Android

This policy explains how Hanabi: Light Up the Sky ("Hanabi") handles information on Android. You do not need an account, and your game progress is saved on your device. The app includes advertising components, uses Google Firebase for analytics and remote configuration, and reports installations and selected gameplay and advertising events to Yideng, AppsFlyer, and Meta so we can understand how the game is found and used. It has no in-app purchases.

Information Handled by the App

Hanabi does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, precise location, photos, contacts, microphone, camera, or payment card details.

To provide the game, Hanabi stores game state and preferences on your device. This includes the night you have reached, in-game item counts such as hints and brooms, the fireworks you have unlocked, tutorials completed, music, sound, haptic and soft-fireworks settings, whether you have seen the first-launch privacy notice, and the level pack downloaded for your session.

Hanabi creates a random app-generated identifier (CUID) and also uses identifiers provided by the operating system and the services described below. These identifiers do not require an account or contain your name:

  • Hanabi CUID. A persistent identifier generated from a random value, used to associate installation and event reports with the same app instance. It is also provided to AppsFlyer as a customer user ID. It is not a hardware serial number or a login credential. On Android it is stored in app-local storage. Device backup and restore may preserve it when the app is reinstalled.
  • Android Advertising ID. A resettable identifier provided by Google Play services. The Firebase, AppLovin, AppsFlyer, and Meta SDKs in the app may read it for analytics, advertising, and measurement, as described below. If you have deleted your Advertising ID, or your device has no Google Play services, no Advertising ID is available.
  • Firebase, AppsFlyer, and Meta identifiers. Random identifiers created by the Google Firebase, AppsFlyer, and Meta SDKs inside the app (Firebase installation and app instance IDs, the AppsFlyer ID, and the Meta anonymous ID). These can also be included in Yideng reports to associate measurement records with the same installation.

Along with these identifiers, the services described below receive technical information about your device and the app: device model and manufacturer, Android version, screen size, app version and version code, language and region settings, time zone, network connection status, and the IP address your device uses to reach the internet.

Analytics

Hanabi includes Google Firebase Analytics. The Firebase integration records automatic events, such as the first time the app is opened, sessions and engagement time, app updates and removals, operating-system updates, and in-app ad impressions once ads are shown. These are tied to the Firebase app instance ID and, where available, the Advertising ID. Firebase may also use Android's privacy-preserving attribution and ad-topic interfaces where your device supports them. Custom events sent to Yideng, AppsFlyer, and Meta are described below.

The app does not include a separate game crash-reporting service. Bundled advertising SDKs may process technical diagnostics, such as errors and crash or performance information, to operate their services.

Google processes this data as our service provider under the Firebase privacy documentation and the Google Privacy Policy.

Advertising and the Advertising ID

Hanabi is free to play and supported by in-app advertising. The current Android version includes rewarded video, interstitial, and banner ads through AppLovin MAX. Whether an ad is shown depends on availability, your progress, and the active advertising configuration.

  • Rewarded ads. You can choose to watch an ad to restore your sparks after a failed night, or receive an additional hint or broom when you have run out. Rewards are granted when the advertising SDK reports that the reward was earned. An unavailable, failed, or dismissed rewarded video does not grant the reward.
  • Interstitial ads. Full-screen ads may appear when you continue after clearing a night, restart after a failed night, or restart from the pause menu. Eligibility depends on progress, that day's completed nights, and cooldowns; an ad is not shown after every action or every night.
  • Banner ads. Banner ads are disabled by default in version 1.0.3 and in the current remote configuration. If enabled remotely, banners may appear during gameplay after the configured night; home-screen banners can also be enabled. Remote settings may change without an app update.

Firebase Remote Config controls advertising switches, eligible nights, frequency, cooldowns, and whether a ready interstitial may substitute for an unavailable rewarded video after you choose to request a reward. This substitution is off by default; if enabled, the reward is granted when the substitute ad closes. These settings may change without an app update. Advertising SDKs initialize when the app launches, including before an ad placement becomes eligible to appear.

Ads are delivered through AppLovin MAX, which mediates demand from several advertising networks: AppLovin, Google AdMob, Unity Ads, Vungle (Liftoff), DT Exchange (Digital Turbine), Mintegral, Pangle (ByteDance), BidMachine, and ironSource (Unity LevelPlay). Some of these networks may not be enabled at a given time, but their software components ship with the app.

To select, deliver, and measure ads, these providers process device and usage information. This typically includes the Android Advertising ID, IP address and the coarse location derived from it, device model and Android version, network and locale information, and your interactions with the ads shown. We do not send them your game saves or your support email. AppLovin also reports the estimated revenue of each impression back to the app, which may be shared with the measurement services in the next section.

Each advertising network handles the data it receives as an independent controller under its own privacy policy, including AppLovin and Unity. AppLovin publishes the current list of its mediation and demand partners on its privacy page.

Install Measurement and Attribution

Hanabi uses Yideng, AppsFlyer, and Meta so that we can count installs, understand which advertising campaign or marketing channel a player came from, and measure advertising performance in aggregate.

Yideng (一灯) measurement. Hanabi sends installation and selected event reports to our Hanabi measurement endpoint at hanabi.colorpro.com.ai, integrated with Yideng. Reports include the CUID; available Firebase installation and app instance IDs, AppsFlyer ID, and Meta anonymous ID; the advertising identifier when available and permitted; app version, platform and distribution channel; device model, screen size, operating-system version, language, region settings, and time zone. The server also receives the IP address used for the request. Region settings are not precise location data.

The reported events are app opens, tutorial completion, and ad impressions. Ad reports contain the ad format (rewarded, interstitial, or banner), currency, and estimated revenue, including cumulative revenue milestones. These reports help measure acquisition and advertising performance and identify invalid reporting. They do not include the full game save, your name, contacts, or support emails. Reports waiting for delivery are stored locally and retried when a connection is available. Deleting or denying an advertising identifier does not stop reports that use the app's CUID and other available installation identifiers.

AppsFlyer. The AppsFlyer SDK starts after the advertising SDK initializes. It receives the AppsFlyer ID, the Android Advertising ID where available, the install referrer from Google Play (and, on some devices, from the Samsung or Huawei app stores), which tells it which store link or campaign led to the install, IP address, device and app information, the CUID, app launches and level-completion events, and, once ads are shown, ad impression revenue with ad format, placement, network, and available creative and revenue-precision data. AppsFlyer matches this against ad campaigns to attribute the install, and processes the data as our service provider under the AppsFlyer Services Privacy Policy.

Meta (Facebook) App Events. The Meta SDK starts after the advertising SDK initializes and records app activations, app-open and level-completion events, so that we can measure installs and engagement from ads we run on Facebook and Instagram. It receives the Meta anonymous ID, the Android Advertising ID where available, device and app information, and IP address. The app does not use Facebook Login and does not access your Facebook account. Meta handles this data under the Meta Privacy Policy.

Remote Configuration and Level Downloads

The app uses Firebase Remote Config to fetch settings such as which level catalog to use, how often new fireworks unlock, and the advertising settings described above. Fetching configuration sends installation identifiers and basic app and device information to Google. If configuration cannot be fetched, the app uses previously activated or built-in settings.

When the configuration points to a newer level pack, the app downloads it from Google Cloud Storage and checks its integrity before installing it on your device. The download is an ordinary web request that includes your IP address; it carries no identifier and no game data.

Your Choices

  • Rewarded ads are optional. You choose whether to request an ad for a reward, including any configured substitute ad described above. Eligible interstitial ads and remotely enabled banners are shown automatically. The game has no player-facing ad-disable setting or paid ad-removal option.
  • Advertising ID controls. In Android Settings you can reset your Advertising ID, or delete it so apps receive no advertising identifier at all, under Settings › Privacy › Ads (the exact path varies by device). Once it is deleted, Firebase, AppLovin, AppsFlyer, and Meta no longer receive it.
  • Clearing app data. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling Hanabi removes your local game saves. Because there is no cloud save, this removes your progress permanently, unless Android restores the app's data from your device backup when you reinstall.
  • Requests about your data. Email us to ask about, or request deletion of, data associated with your installation. Because we do not hold an account for you, tell us the device and approximate install date so we can work with our providers to find the right records.

Android Permissions and Haptics

Hanabi may use your device's haptic feedback for taps and launches. This does not provide us with personal information, and you can turn haptics off in the game's settings.

The app declares these permissions: internet and network-state access to load ads, fetch configuration and level packs, and send the reports described above; the Advertising ID permission and Android's ad-services permissions for privacy-preserving attribution and ad topics, declared by the bundled analytics, advertising, and measurement components; access to the install referrer services of Google Play and some device-maker app stores; and a wake lock so network work can finish. The current version does not request access to precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or notifications.

No In-App Purchases

Hanabi does not offer in-app purchases. Hints and brooms may be granted during their tutorials or obtained through optional rewarded ads; they cannot be bought. The app does not use Google Play Billing and does not handle payment information.

Support and External Links

When you choose Support, Hanabi can open an email app with a draft addressed to us. We receive only the information you decide to include and send. Your email provider processes the message under its own terms.

The app may also open legal pages in your browser, and tapping an ad will open a destination chosen by the advertiser. Once you leave the app, the privacy practices of the destination website and browser apply.

How Information Is Used

Local game data is used only to provide game features: resume progress, keep your hints and brooms, remember unlocked fireworks, tutorials and settings, and play the downloaded level pack.

Analytics, advertising, configuration, and measurement data are used to operate and improve the game: to understand how the game is used, deliver and cap ads, measure ad performance and revenue, tune level pacing, count installs and attribute them to ad campaigns, and detect invalid or fraudulent activity.

Information you voluntarily send to support is used to answer your request, troubleshoot issues, and protect the game and its players from abuse.

Sharing and Sale of Information

We do not sell or rent player personal information, and we do not share your game saves or support correspondence with advertisers. Data is shared only as described in this policy: with Google for analytics, remote configuration, and level downloads; with AppLovin and the mediated advertising networks to deliver and measure ads; through our Yideng-integrated endpoint for installation, tutorial and advertising-event measurement; with AppsFlyer and Meta for installation attribution and event measurement; when you choose to contact support; when required by law; or when necessary to protect rights, safety, and security.

Some privacy laws treat sharing advertising identifiers with advertising networks for personalized advertising as a "sale" or "share" of personal information. The simplest way to stop Advertising ID-based sharing is to delete your Advertising ID as described in Your Choices.

Retention and Deletion

CUID and queued reports are stored on your device. Clearing app storage removes local queued reports and the local CUID, but backups may restore identifiers and uninstalling does not erase server-side records. Measurement records are retained by us and our service providers as needed for the purposes described in this policy and applicable obligations. Contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of associated records; removing an advertising identifier does not delete data previously reported.

Local game data remains on your device until the app overwrites it, you clear the app's storage, or you uninstall the app. Hanabi does not provide cloud save, so clearing storage or uninstalling may permanently remove local progress.

Analytics and measurement data are retained by us and our providers only as long as needed for the purposes above, according to each provider's retention settings and applicable law, and are then deleted or aggregated so that they no longer identify a single installation.

Support emails are retained only as reasonably necessary to respond, keep support records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. You may contact us to request deletion of information you previously provided through support, subject to applicable law.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information handled by the app. Configuration, level downloads, and measurement reports are sent over encrypted connections, and downloaded level packs are checked against a fingerprint before they are installed. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure, so avoid including sensitive personal information in support messages unless necessary.

Children's Privacy

Hanabi is a general-audience puzzle game. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game has no accounts and no social features, but it does include advertising, analytics, and install measurement as described above, so it is not intended for use by children under the age at which consent is required in your country. If you believe a child has used the app or sent personal information through support, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when Hanabi or legal requirements change. The latest version will be posted here with a revised date. If a future release adds gameplay analytics, crash reporting, notifications, purchases, cloud saves, accounts, or another data service, this policy will be updated before release.
